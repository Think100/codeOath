# Welcome

This is the first post on my new Markdown-powered blog. Everything you see here
started as a plain `.md` file in the `posts/` folder.

## Why Markdown?

Writing in Markdown means I can focus on the words instead of fighting with HTML
tags. A few examples of what works out of the box:

- **Bold** and *italic* text
- [Links](https://www.markdownguide.org/)
- Lists, like this one

> Blockquotes are handy for pulling out a key idea.

That is all it takes to publish a post.
